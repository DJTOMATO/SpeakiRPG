Speaki RPG Desktop v1.2.0 is Here!

A huge cinematic and UI overhaul is now available! Explore the world with a brand new stabilized Drone Camera and cinematic filters.
  • Advanced Drone Camera: Explore the map with complete freedom using the new stabilized Free Camera mode (press F4 to toggle, WASD to fly, Space/Ctrl for elevation).
  • Cinematic Camera Filters: Instantly switch between 5 visual styles (Ctrl + 6~0 for Normal, B&W, Sepia, Morning, Dusk).
  • Dynamic UI Scaling: Shrink the HUD without squishing the 3D game world! Use /uiscale 0.5 - 2.0 in chat or Ctrl + U to reset.
  • Hotkeys Viewer Modal: Easily check all available mod shortcuts directly in-game by clicking the new ⌨️ icon.
  • Native Night Mode Toggle: Quickly toggle the game's darker scenery mode by pressing N.
